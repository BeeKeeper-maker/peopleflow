# PeopleFlow Competitor Research Plan

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

## Purpose
Before major section-by-section upgrades, analyze strong HR/HRMS/attendance/payroll products so PeopleFlow improves from market evidence, not guesses. The goal is not copying; the goal is understanding best patterns, local gaps, and differentiation opportunities for Bangladeshi offices.

## Competitor Groups

### Global HR / HCM Leaders
- BambooHR — SMB HRIS, onboarding, employee records, leave, performance.
- Zoho People — affordable HR suite, attendance, geo/IP fencing, mobile, Zoho ecosystem.
- Rippling — HR + IT + automation, deep workflows, strong scaling pattern.
- Gusto — small business payroll + HR simplicity.
- ADP / Paycor / Paychex — payroll, compliance, attendance, reporting, reliability.
- Workday — enterprise HCM, analytics, scale, complex org structures.
- Deel — global HR/payroll and contractor workflows.

### Attendance / Leave Specialists
- AttendanceBot — Slack/Teams-based leave and attendance workflow.
- Vacation Tracker — leave approval, policies, calendar/payroll integration.
- Homebase / When I Work — hourly workforce scheduling, time clocks, mobile.
- Open HRMS/Odoo — modular ERP-style HR and biometric integration patterns.

### Bangladesh / Regional Competitors
- AccordHRM — HR/payroll, leave, attendance, biometric/GPS, shifts/overtime, reports.
- GreenHR — Bangladesh HR/payroll, attendance, cloud/mobile positioning.
- Smart HRM — garments/corporate/factory HR, payroll, shift, overtime, leave.
- Kormee HRM — customizable HR/payroll and attendance workflows.
- PiHR / PeopleDesk / Southtech / LinesPay / Mango HR / Systech iHR — local HR/payroll ecosystem to inspect.

## Research Dimensions
For each competitor/product, capture:

1. Target customer and positioning
2. Core modules
3. Onboarding/setup experience
4. Employee self-service UX
5. Leave/attendance workflow
6. Payroll and compliance model
7. Biometric/GPS/mobile attendance approach
8. Reporting/analytics depth
9. RBAC/security/data isolation claims
10. Pricing/packaging strategy
11. Mobile app support
12. Integrations/API ecosystem
13. Strengths PeopleFlow should learn from
14. Weaknesses/gaps PeopleFlow can beat
15. Ideas for Bangladesh-specific differentiation

## Early Findings
- Global leaders win through polished onboarding, self-service, workflow automation, integrations, and reliable payroll/compliance.
- Bangladesh competitors emphasize payroll, attendance, leave, shifts, overtime, biometric/GPS tracking, and local office/factory fit.
- Strong opportunity for PeopleFlow: combine local Bangladesh office realities with cleaner UX, stronger tenant/security architecture, better Bengali experience, transparent SaaS packaging, and a practical Sync Agent/device strategy.
